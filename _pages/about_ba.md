### Dr Becky Allen - Project Lead

Becky is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science. Her research primarily sits within music technology, with specific interests in the use of sound / music for community wellbeing.  Becky is also interested in the use of sonification for engagement with AI literacy, complex datasets and science communication.

Becky is also a co-founder of the North East Sound Women Network which is a community for gender minorities who are interested or work in all aspects of audio.
