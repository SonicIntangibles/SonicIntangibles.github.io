### Dr George Caselton - Research Assistant

George is a Research Assistant at Newcastle University working on the Audio Universe project. With an academic background in both Music and Computer Science, he works primarily as a Software Engineer building a Sonification Suite for Planetaria. The Suite is a web app designed for planetarium staff and science communicators, enabling them to easily create sonic representations of astronomical phenomena, with the aim of making their planetarium content more accessible, immersive, and engaging.
